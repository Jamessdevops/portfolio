Privacy Policy

Habit Tracker for iOS · Last updated 24 September 2026

Habit Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not contain any networking code, so your data has no way to leave your device.

What stays on your device

The habits you create, their schedules and icons, and your daily completion history are saved in a private container on your iPhone. This container is shared only between Habit Tracker and its own home screen widget. It is not readable by other apps, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

If you use iCloud or encrypted iTunes/Finder backups, your device backup may include this data. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Purchases

Habit Tracker offers an optional Pro subscription and a one-time lifetime unlock. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, card number, or Apple Account credentials. The app only asks Apple whether a valid purchase exists, and stores that yes-or-no answer on your device to unlock features.

Tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because there is nothing to track. No data is shared with data brokers, advertisers, or any other third party.

Children

Habit Tracker is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of your habits and history from your device permanently. There is no server-side copy for us to delete, because none was ever created.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
